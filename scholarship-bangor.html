About this scholarship

The Vice-Chancellor Scholarship is a tuition fee discount for outstanding international students starting an eligible on-campus programme at Bangor University. The award is £10,000 for the first year.

For January 2027 entry, the university lists one undergraduate award and one postgraduate taught award. You need an offer from Bangor before you can submit the scholarship application.

Who can apply

  • Be an international applicant paying international tuition fees.
  • Hold an offer to study an eligible full-time, on-campus Bangor course starting in January 2027.
  • Undergraduate applicants should have excellent academic results and notable extracurricular achievements.
  • Postgraduate taught applicants should have an excellent academic record, demonstrate commitment to sustainable development, and be self funded.

The award applies to the first year only and cannot be combined with another Bangor University scholarship for that year.

How to apply

  1. Apply to Bangor University for an eligible course and receive an offer.
  2. Review the criteria on Bangor’s official page, then use its scholarship application form.
  3. Submit by 11 December 2026.

Successful applicants for the January intake are expected to be informed by the beginning of January 2027. Review the official scholarship details.
