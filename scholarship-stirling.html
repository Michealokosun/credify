Postgraduate Nigeria
Scholarship

A tuition fee waiver for eligible students living in Nigeria who plan to study a full-time, on-campus postgraduate taught course at Stirling.

About this scholarship

The University of Stirling Postgraduate Nigeria Scholarship offers a £4,000 tuition fee waiver. The award is intended for international students who are living in Nigeria when they apply.

For January 2027 entry, submit your eligible course application within the published application window. Stirling identifies eligible students through the admissions process, so there is no separate scholarship application.

Who can apply

  • Be living in Nigeria at the point of application.
  • Be classed as an international student for tuition fee purposes.
  • Hold a conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible postgraduate taught course starting in January 2027.
  • Study full time, on campus, and be self funded.

Award details

AWARD£4,000 tuition fee waiver
STUDY LEVELPostgraduate taught
ENTRYJanuary 2027
HOW TO BE CONSIDEREDAutomatic during admissions

The award cannot be combined with other Stirling scholarships, except for the university’s Sports Scholarships. If selected, a £1,000 deposit is required by the deadline in the offer letter.

How to apply

  1. Choose an eligible full-time postgraduate taught course at Stirling.
  2. Apply for admission by 31 October 2026 for the January 2027 entry window.
  3. If you meet the criteria, Stirling will identify you during admissions and send a scholarship offer.
