Postgraduate Taught
Scholarship

A first-year tuition fee discount for eligible Nigerian students enrolling in selected University of Exeter master’s programmes.

About this scholarship

The University of Exeter Postgraduate Taught Scholarship for Nigeria offers a £10,000 reduction in first-year tuition fees for eligible students entering in the 2026/27 academic year.

For January 2027 entry, the award is available only for a defined list of postgraduate taught courses, including selected programmes in business, finance, management, computer and data science, and engineering.

Who can apply

  • Be a Nigerian citizen or passport holder and classed as an international student for fee purposes.
  • Hold an offer for an eligible postgraduate taught master’s course beginning in the 2026/27 academic year.
  • Meet the conditions in your offer and pay any required tuition fee deposit.
  • Be a self-funded student.

Award details

AWARD£10,000 fee discount
STUDY LEVELPostgraduate taught master’s
ENTRYJanuary 2027
HOW TO BE CONSIDEREDAutomatic for eligible applicants

The scholarship is applied to first-year tuition fees and is not repeated in later years. It may be combined with an Exeter Excellence Scholarship, up to a maximum combined value of £10,000.

How to apply

  1. Check that your intended January 2027 course is included in Exeter’s eligible January-start course list.
  2. Apply for the postgraduate course and receive an offer.
  3. Meet the offer conditions and submit any required deposit by the scholarship deadline, 1 December 2026.

Eligible applicants are considered automatically; a separate scholarship application is not required. Review the official award details.
